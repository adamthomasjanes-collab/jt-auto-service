The problem is unclear

DON’T GUESS AT THE PART.NOTICE THE PATTERN.

A warning light is useful, but the story around it can be just as important. The goal is not for you to diagnose the car. It is to give J&T a useful place to begin.

01WHAT CHANGED?

Starting, idle, acceleration, sound, smell, vibration or something that simply feels different.

02WHEN DOES IT HAPPEN?

Cold start, after sitting, highway speed, after rain, under load or only once in a while.

03WHAT DOES THE DASH SAY?

Note the warning light or message. A photo can help if it disappears later.

04WHAT ELSE CHANGED?

Weak starts, recent work, fluid loss or another symptom that arrived around the same time.

Diagnostics FAQ

BEFORE YOUSCHEDULE.

What makes a symptom useful for diagnostics?

Specific context helps: what the vehicle did, when it happened, how often it happens, and what else changed around the same time.

Does a warning light tell me exactly what is wrong?

Not necessarily. A warning light or code is a starting clue; the symptom and inspection provide the context needed to understand what is happening.

Do I need to know the trouble code?

No. If you have a code or photo of a warning message, bring it, but your description of the symptom is enough to begin the conversation.
