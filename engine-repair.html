When the engine runs differently

TELL US HOW IT CHANGED.NOT WHAT YOU THINK FAILED.

Rough running, loss of power, unusual noise, smoke, smell, or a warning light can all be useful. The pattern matters more than guessing which engine part caused it.

  • Rough running or misfire feeling
  • Loss of power
  • Unusual engine noise
  • Smoke, odor or warning light
Describe the symptom

Whether the problem appears at idle, under acceleration, on a cold start, or after the engine warms up gives the shop better context.

Engine Repair FAQ

BEFORE YOUSCHEDULE.

What engine symptoms are worth mentioning?

Any new change in power, idle, acceleration, sound, smell, temperature, smoke, leaking, or warning lights is useful context.

What should I do if the engine is overheating?

If the temperature is climbing or the vehicle is overheating, stop driving when it is safe to do so and call the shop for guidance.

What should I have ready when I call about an engine problem?

Have the year, make and model ready and describe when the engine acts differently—especially whether it happens cold, warm, at idle, or under acceleration.
